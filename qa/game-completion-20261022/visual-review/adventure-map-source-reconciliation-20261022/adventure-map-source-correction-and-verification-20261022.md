## Gameplay and save integrity

- tests/adventure-worlds.test.mjs checks all 12 worlds and each of their 20 deterministic layouts (240 total): safe starts, walkable resource anchors, reachable sites and guards, non-overlapping site entrances, real bridge decks with contiguous open banks, and terrain blocks. Astral Peaks gold is now walkable at cell [13,5] and remains reachable in every layout.
- The visible isolated-browser journey used the ordinary controls in Sunlit Riverlands: traveled to a Shrine and learned its lesson, crossed a registered bridge, collected the Food cache, claimed Mine gold, fought and won a seeded guard encounter, received +150 gold/+110 XP, returned to Adventure, saved, reloaded, and continued with the rewards, XP, and 17 surviving units intact.
- Functional map interactions remain state-driven. Hero travel/mount placement follows saved campaign state; caches and guards use deterministic seed placements plus collection/resolution state; resolved guards disappear; site hotspots use the registered site and approach geometry and report claimed state. Background landmarks are scenery, not ownership state. Where a painted site was insufficient, versioned runtime-object art stays tied to that actual site interaction.
- Save migration now accepts Adventure geometry versions 1 and 2, retains stable world IDs and progress, and writes version 3. It relocates a saved hero only when the old cell is blocked or disconnected and preserves discoveries, claims, rewards, resolved guards, and pending battle packs. Present malformed versions remain errors instead of triggering a reset. Only the isolated test origin on port 4174 was used; the existing origin on port 4173 and its saves were not opened or modified.
