These are current camera/rendering measurements only; full game-specific map composition, readability, hazards, and correct geometry must be checked after reconciliation.
3. **Adventure production maps and geometry — INCOMPLETE.** Owner clarification says the supplied paintings are examples. Eleven current runtime bindings point at those references; the remaining generated map also lacks a fresh scene-and-geometry acceptance here. Do not bind the 12th reference to make counts match. Design/verify game-specific terrain with site, route, obstacle, bridge and collision alignment; preserve the 12 current one-move journeys only as runtime evidence.
4. **Story at 825×375 — responsive layout OPEN; conflicting live measurements.** Fresh isolated menu-to-Story runs02/03 at CSS viewport 825×375 measure lower rival controls y=245–326 against navigation y=315–363, with seven intersecting pairs. The other same-day local-origin review's 825×375 screenshot/summary records a lower-control edge y=309.35 and navigation start y=311.60, a 2.25px gap. Both audits record the same current `main.js`, `game.css` and `polish.css` hashes. At 933×424 and 1280×720, this review measured no overlap. Do not claim the defect closed or universal from either sample; reproduce with matched browser zoom, font metrics and layout timing. Rival/chapter selection still worked and persisted after reload.
5. **Defense preparation — visual FAIL/OPEN.** `Start Campaign Siege` is simultaneously visible as a bottom-left quick action and as the primary button in the preparation panel at both reviewed sizes. At 1280×720, the second tower-purchase row is only partly visible at the panel's initial scroll position; verify its scroll and focus path after layout repair.
6. **Campaign Siege natural victory — NOT REACHED.** Both the default two-tower roster and a naturally purchased third Support tower lost at wave 2/5. Loss settlement and durable reload behaved consistently. A route that naturally acquires enough stone for Splash and uses the Hero's blast remains untested; do not infer a balance defect or claim campaign-siege completion from these losses.
7. **Broader acceptance remains open.** Actual screens and journeys still need full comparison against the advanced mock references and current owner contracts. Passing route scripts, tests, and contact sheets do not close actor/gear/motion, all-age layout, natural journey, save/recovery, or scene acceptance.
