## Design review and acceptance limits

Existing matching components can be useful, while the clean state-aware landform, upper citadel/perimeter registration and broad articulated motion remain unproven in an actual game.

The design gallery and its browser screenshots are **proposals for section feedback**, not a playable game, accepted likeness or proof of dynamic construction/combat.
