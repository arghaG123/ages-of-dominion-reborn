## Research and decision

The publisher's [Heroes III manual, page44](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/297000/manuals/BONUS_Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_III_HDEdition_OldManual1999_EN.pdf?t=1699009789) documents positive morale as a chance to act again. Its [HTML reproduction](https://manualmachine.com/gamespc/heroesofmightandmagiciii/1119554-user-manual/) was also inspected. The [Heroes V manual, Luck and Morale](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/15170/manuals/manual_english.pdf?t=1447351319) describes faster return to action through its initiative cycle. These are different published designs, not a universal professional-game rule.

The reborn Tactical engine uses rounds and an action queue, rather than Heroes V's continuous initiative cycle. I choose a **real immediate bonus stack action**, with strict one-per-round/no-chain limits. This makes the frozen Leadership description functional. Numeric probability retains the previous game's actual coefficient `positiveMorale * 0.042` (read-only `C:/dev/ages-of-dominion/src/battle/index.js:568`), which previously produced only a flash/log. Thus the exact reborn probabilities are4.2%,8.4%,12.6% for morale1,2,3. These are our adopted reference-compatible values, not a claim that every published Heroes edition uses those exact rates.

This is an authorized design change from cosmetic inspiration to a functional action. The public manuals support the general mechanic; trigger exclusions, deterministic storage and the anti-chain rule below are this game's explicit design choices. Negative morale skipping, race mixing penalties and imported undead/elemental immunity rules are not part of this positive-morale decision.

## Exact execution contract

- Scope: round-based Tactical combat, including campaign encounters, Duel and isolated Skirmish/Challenge when their scenario specifies a commander/stack morale value. Real-time Siege/Endless has no discrete bonus turns; show Tactical-only applicability there and do not invent faster firing/damage/income bonuses.
- Effective morale: use earned commander Leadership0..3 and supported equipped artifact bonuses (including Ancient Banner), supported stack modifiers and documented weather penalty; clamp to[-3,3]. No generated-art statistic, debug grant or undocumented composition bonus. Pass the actual morale into battle snapshots; current encounter/Duel constructors omit it. Default absent commander/stack modifiers to0; do not invent an enemy commander or immunities for a hero class/creature identity without data authority.
- Probability: `p = max(0, effectiveMorale) * 0.042`; effective morale is capped3, so p<=.126. Values<=0 have no bonus and consume no morale RNG draw. Do not add negative turn skips under this decision.
- Trigger: exactly once after a stack's first eligible completed ordinary Move, Melee or Shoot activation in a round, after damage/retaliation and outcome resolution. The acting stack must still be alive and battle ACTIVE. Wait only defers activation; Defend, commander Spell, retaliation, rejected actions and bonus actions do not trigger a morale roll. No roll after the battle resolves. A stack that consumes its ordinary turn on Defend has no morale bonus that round.
- On success: persist the consumed roll and grant that same stack one immediate bonus activation ahead of the next ordinary stack. Display “High morale — extra action” and the actual chance. Bonus activation can Move/Melee/Shoot/Defend or use an explicit Skip bonus action. Wait is unavailable during the bonus; explain why. No bonus activation may generate another morale roll or further bonus.
- Resources/turn limits: normal movement/range/target legality, current counts, ammunition and costs still apply. No healing, stat boost, casualty reset, free mana or rewards. Do not reset once-per-round spell/retaliation/Wait rights or buff/debuff counters. Existing action-based effect decay occurs on each completed applicable activation, including the bonus; it is not refreshed by inspiration.
- Determinism: consume exactly one persisted battle RNG draw per eligible check in stable order and log round/stack/morale/chance/roll/outcome. Persist check-consumed, bonus-granted/used and current bonus activation state atomically with the completed command. Render/preview/load/replay must not reroll. Keep queue IDs unique; model activation state without invalid duplicate stack IDs or another ordinary turn. Reset per-round eligibility only on a genuine next-round transition.
- Auto/opponent: use the same legal resolver and consume the same kind of activation/RNG as manual commands. Each side uses its own documented morale value, not the player's hero for an enemy. Practice morale belongs to its isolated battle snapshot and never modifies campaign RNG/rewards/story/items/mana.
- Save compatibility: old saves initialize missing morale bookkeeping conservatively. A stack already marked acted in a legacy active round cannot receive a retroactive roll/bonus; unacted stacks remain eligible when they actually complete an action. New saves validate flags, round consistency, active bonus identity/aliveness, queue uniqueness and finite morale/chance values. Same command replay or reload cannot duplicate a bonus.
