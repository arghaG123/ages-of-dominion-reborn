## The concrete local solution

The consolidated Image brief supplies exact current inputs/output paths, four native source hashes, conservative Healer native locator boxes, per-ID decisions, a fixed order and attempt counts. It corrects four specific method errors:

1. **Background connectivity is not anatomical connectivity.** A magenta perimeter/green quadrant can remain connected while painted limbs are separate. Segment one anatomical ROI with protected subject seeds and explicit within-panel background; do not rank connected components by size and name them by aspect ratio.
2. **Color similarity is not a mask.** Native boot/leather/soil, helmet highlights/magenta and glow/background collisions require contour-aware protected foreground. Keep good RGB under the alpha. No repeat horizontal cuts, erosions, blanket keys, blur or fabricated anatomy. At most two local mask candidates per unresolved ROI; retain rejected attempts as QA.
3. **Crop-edge abutment is not an anatomical joint.** Place the child cuff under an actual sleeve/armor lip with the pivot inside painted material and natural z-order. One bounded12-placement search with fixed joint coordinates at -25/0/+25°, plus one correction. Observe contour/contact at actual screen scale; a one-pixel touch fails. A universal >40-native-pixel overlap threshold does not by itself prove a natural socket.
4. **The old clipping measurement is invalid.** `scripts/image_v4_rigs.py` rotates into the unchanged child crop and counts child pixels outside a dilated parent as clipping. A limb legitimately extends beyond its parent. Use padded transformed bounds, independent masks, local joint overlap and actual out-of-canvas loss; compare against the same padded resampling reference. Code owns final skeleton and running motion.

This is a reproducible method proposal, **not an already performed v6 repair or a guaranteed perfect result**. Neutral poses/limited joints can pass individually while wider rotations/absent anatomy remain incomplete. Preserving a combined staff/hand arm can be more useful than inventing a hidden elbow. Whole-game camera/scene/depth/HUD/scale remain Code deliverables.

The source-to-mask-to-derivative recipe must record native crop bounds, mask hashes, affine transforms, source/derivative landmarks, anatomical side uncertainty and actual allowed consumer size/rotation. Current v5 hash-only additions do not supply all of that reproducibility. Keep native-matte, static-scale, rig, spatial, runtime and owner statuses separate.
