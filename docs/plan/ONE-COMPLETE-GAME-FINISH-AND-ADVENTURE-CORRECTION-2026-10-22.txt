### Coordinate example — avoid applying the native scale twice

The following is an illustrative calculation, not a new accepted camera or a moved game anchor. Let `A` be world→1376×768 stage, `S` be stage→native-image scaling and `C` be stage→viewport camera. Then:

```text
world p → stage u = A*p
native image sample n = S*u
viewport v = C*u = C*inverse(S)*n
pointer v → world p = inverse(A)*inverse(C)*v
```

Example: Kingdom point `(8,8)` maps under the existing affine to stage `(850,365)`. A 5504×3072 source is 4× the stage, so the corresponding native sample is `(3400,1460)`. With an EXAMPLE uniform camera scale .5 and translation `(10,20)`, viewport point is `(435,202.5)`, not a 4× shifted actor. If the native terrain image is drawn into a 1376×768 stage box, overlay the actor at `(850,365)` before applying the same camera; do not use native `(3400,1460)` as the SVG-stage actor coordinate. Test forward/inverse identity and actual bridge/foot contacts after resize.
